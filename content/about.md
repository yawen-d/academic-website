Hello! I'm Alice Wu, a professor of artificial intelligence at Stanford University. My work explores the intersection of machine learning, embodied intelligence, and human language, with a focus on building systems that can reason about complex environments and communicate what they learn.

## Research focus

- **Embodied learning.** Designing adaptive control policies for self-reconfiguring robots and autonomous agents.
- **Language and reasoning.** Studying how large language models ground knowledge in physical tasks and collaborate with humans.
- **Responsible AI.** Developing transparent evaluation frameworks that connect technical progress with social impact.

## Teaching and mentorship

I teach graduate- and undergraduate-level courses on machine learning theory, robotics, and natural language processing. In the Robotic Neurobiology group, I mentor students across disciplines as we explore new ways for intelligent systems to sense, decide, and act.

## Beyond the lab

I share essays, reading notes, and tutorials on the blog to make advanced ideas accessible. When I'm away from the keyboard, you'll often find me hiking coastal trails or experimenting with analog photography.
